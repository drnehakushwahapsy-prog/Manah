Frequently asked questions

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. What you share stays private, within the limits of safety and the law. We will talk through this in your first session.

How do I know which service is right for me?

Book a first session. We will talk about your concerns and decide together which approach suits you.

Can I have sessions online?

Yes. Most individual, couple and child counselling sessions are available online as well as in person.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to reschedule or cancel a session.

How do I pay?

Payment is made at or before each session. Accepted methods will be confirmed when you book.
